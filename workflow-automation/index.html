Automation

Workflow automation for quotes, invoices, follow-ups and reports

The repetitive work that fills your week, done in the background the same way every time, so your team spends its hours on work that needs a person.

What it covers

What we build for you

  • Quotes

    An enquiry comes in, the details are collected and a draft quote is prepared from your price list, ready for your approval.

  • Invoices and reminders

    Invoices go out when a job is marked done, and polite reminders follow until it is paid.

  • Follow-up sequences

    Every lead gets a reply and a follow-up schedule, so enquiries stop going quiet because nobody had time.

  • Client onboarding

    Welcome emails, intake forms, document requests and setup tasks start the moment someone signs.

  • Weekly reporting

    Numbers from your tools pulled into one summary and sent to you every Monday morning.

  • Data entry

    Details from emails, forms and PDFs copied into your CRM or spreadsheet without anyone retyping them.

Signs it is time to automate

If any of these sound familiar, there is probably a few hours a week to win back. The free audit puts a number on it.

  • Someone copies the same information between two systems
  • New leads wait more than an hour for a reply
  • Invoices go out late because someone has to remember
  • Your weekly report takes half a day to put together
  • Onboarding a new client means the same ten emails every time

How it works

How we set it up

  1. 01 · MAP

    Map

    We walk through the workflow with the people who do it today and write down every step, tool and exception.

  2. 02 · BUILD

    Build

Questions

Workflow Automation: common questions

More answers on the FAQ page →

Which tools can you automate?

Most tools with an API or email access. We check your exact setup during the free audit before quoting anything.

What if something breaks?

We monitor every automation after launch. If a connected app changes how it works, we update the automation as part of support.

Do we need to replace our software?

Usually not. We build around what you already use, and only suggest a change when it clearly saves money or time.
