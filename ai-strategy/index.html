What it covers

What we build for you

  • Free AI audit

    A working session on your real workflows. You leave with a list of where AI saves time or wins leads, ranked by return, with scope and fixed pricing.

  • Roadmap

    A sequenced plan: what to build first, what to build next and what to leave alone for now.

Questions

AI Strategy & Training: common questions

More answers on the FAQ page →

What happens in the free audit?

A working session on your actual workflows. We map where time and leads are being lost, find where AI pays for itself fastest and hand you a plain-English plan with scope and pricing. The plan is yours to keep either way.

Do you train non-technical staff?

Yes. Most sessions are for people who have never written a prompt. We use their real tasks, not generic examples.

Can you write our AI use policy?

Yes. We draft it with you in plain language and adjust it to your industry and the information you handle. In regulated sectors, have your legal advisor review the final version.
