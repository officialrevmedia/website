What MCP is, in plain English

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT connect to outside tools and data through one consistent interface. Instead of a one-off bridge for every app, an MCP server describes what the AI is allowed to read and which actions it may take. You control those permissions.

For a business, that means one assistant your team can ask about clients, schedules, documents and numbers, with each connection scoped and logged.

  • Least access needed for each connection
  • Read-only wherever possible
  • Every action logged
  • Keys kept in accounts you own

Questions

Systems & MCP Integration: common questions

More answers on the FAQ page →

Do I need MCP, or just an integration?

If you want an assistant your team talks to that can look things up and take actions across several tools, MCP is the clean way to do it. For a single fixed workflow, a direct integration is often simpler. We tell you which during the audit.

Which AI models do you work with?

We build with leading models, including Claude and ChatGPT, and choose per project based on the task, cost and data requirements.

Where does my data go?

Connections are scoped to what the job needs and run in accounts you own wherever possible. We document every connection and its access before launch.
