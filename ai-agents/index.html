AI agents that answer, qualify and book, around the clock

An AI agent picks up every call and chat, asks the questions your best receptionist would ask, books the appointment and sends your team a clean summary. Nights, weekends and holidays included.

What it covers

What we build for you

  • Calls and chats, answered

    Phone, website chat, SMS and social messages answered in seconds, using your hours, services and pricing rules.

  • Lead qualification

    The agent asks your screening questions, sorts serious enquiries from tire-kickers and routes the good ones to the right person.

  • Booking and scheduling

    Appointments go straight into your calendar, with confirmations and reminders sent automatically. Reschedules are handled too.

  • After-hours coverage

    Evenings and weekends stop being the time your leads call a competitor instead.

  • Missed-call text back

    If a call is missed, the caller gets a text within seconds and the conversation carries on there.

  • Clean handoff

    When someone needs a person, the agent transfers the call or flags it, with notes so nobody has to repeat themselves.

You stay in control

You decide what the agent is allowed to say, what it can book and when it must hand off to a person. Every conversation is logged, so you can read exactly what was said. If the agent does not know an answer, it takes the caller's details and passes the question to your team. It does not guess.

Questions

AI Agents & Assistants: common questions

More answers on the FAQ page →

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

We recommend the agent says so at the start of the conversation. In our experience people care most about getting a quick, correct answer and a booking.

Can it use my existing phone number?

Yes. Calls can forward to the agent all the time, only after hours, or only when your line is busy. Your number stays the same.

What happens if the agent does not know the answer?

It tells the caller it will check, takes their details and sends the question to your team with a summary. It is set up not to make things up.
