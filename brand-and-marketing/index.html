Questions

Brand & Marketing: common questions

More answers on the FAQ page →

What is included in ads management?

Campaign setup, conversion tracking, keyword and audience research, ad copy and creative, weekly optimization and a monthly report.

Can you refresh my brand instead of starting over?

Yes. Many projects keep what already works, such as the name or a colour, and tighten everything else into one consistent system.

Do you design for print?

Yes. Signage, business cards, menus, packaging and brochures, delivered as print-ready files.
