Straight answers

Asked, answered.

What is the free AI audit?
A working session on your actual workflows. We map where time and leads are being lost, identify where AI pays for itself fastest, and hand you a plain-English plan with scope and pricing. No cost, no obligation, and the plan is yours to keep either way.
How fast can an AI system go live?
Most first systems go live in two to four weeks. We start with the single highest-return workflow, launch it, prove it with real results, then expand from there.
Which industries do you work with?
Any industry. We have built for construction, real estate, clinics, restaurants, professional services, retail, and nonprofits. If your business has repetitive work or unanswered leads, AI applies.
Do I need technical staff to use this?
No. We design, build, host, and maintain everything. Your team gets simple controls and hands-on training. You run your business, and the systems run in the background.
What does AI implementation cost?
Every build is scoped to your business after the free audit, so you get fixed pricing before anything starts. Most clients begin with one high-return system and expand once it proves itself.
Will AI replace my team?
We build AI to take the repetitive work off your team, not to take your team off the payroll. The goal is simple: your people spend their hours on the work that actually needs a human.
