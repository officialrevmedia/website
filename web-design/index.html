Web Design & Development: common questions

How long does a website take?

A one-page starter site can be delivered in 24 hours through 24hourwebsite.ca. Larger custom sites are scoped after a short call, and you get the timeline in writing before we start.

Do you write the content?

Yes. We can write homepage and service copy from a short intake, or work from content you already have.

Can you redesign my current site without losing my Google rankings?

Yes. We keep or redirect your existing URLs, carry over titles and descriptions that already rank, and check Search Console after launch.
